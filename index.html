Ghost Rovers

For ages 8 to 12. Rovers, landers, a helicopter, orbiters, a science station and a descent stage — left behind on the Moon, on Mars and out in deep space. Each machine tells its own story. Pick a world, then tap a glowing signal to hear one.

Moon and Mars maps are real NASA mosaics. Deep Space shows real photos of Ceres and Eros on a drawn star map. Positions are from real landing coordinates where given.

Tap a glowing signal on the map to wake up a machine.

Sources and credits

Every fact comes from the NASA pages linked in each story. Moon and Mars maps are real NASA mosaics; the Deep Space star map is drawn for this project. Map credits are below. Stories are written for ages 8 to 12 in each machine’s imagined voice; the facts are real.

About dates: for Spirit, 22 March 2010 was its last contact and 25 May 2011 was when NASA declared its mission complete. For InSight, 15 December 2022 was its last contact and 21 December 2022 was when NASA declared its mission over. Dawn’s story is short because its source page doesn’t give its ending — we kept it short rather than guessing.

Mars map: NASA Ames / USGS Astrogeology, Viking Orbiter imagery (Mars Viking MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic, centred at 90°E). Moon map: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, LRO WAC nearside mosaic. Ceres photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA, Dawn (bright spots, PIA19604). Eros photo: NASA/JPL/JHUAPL, NEAR Shoemaker (PIA02923). Deep Space star map is drawn for this project.
